# HouseBud tester notice (template)

Send this to each tester before they sign up, with the bracketed parts filled in. Keep a copy of who received it and when, and of any parent's agreement.

---

## Welcome to the HouseBud test

Thanks for trying HouseBud. It's an app for running a shared home: chores, a shopping list, shared expenses, house rules, a calendar and a household chat. You're one of the first people using it for real, so a few things to know first.

**It's a test.** The app works, but expect rough edges, and tell me about them. Features may change, and I may need to fix or reset things. If something important goes wrong (wrong amounts in expenses, for example), let me know rather than working around it.

**What the app stores about you:**

- Your account: email address, username, name, birth date, school and year, the city and ZIP code you're looking in, and your password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it, me included).
- What you add to your profile: a display name, a short bio, a photo link.
- Your answers to the compatibility questions, if you fill them in.
- What you do in your household: chores, shopping items, expenses and payments, house rules and votes, calendar entries, and chat messages.

**Who sees it:**

- **Your household** sees your name and photo, and everything you add in the household (chores, expenses, chat…). People who join later don't see chat messages from before they joined.
- [While matching is closed: **Nobody else** sees your profile.] [Once matching is open: other users looking in the same area see your profile card (name, age, school, photo) and your compatibility with them.]
- **Me**, as the person running the app: I can access the database to fix problems and to take backups. I won't look at your household's content unless you ask me to, or to fix something you reported.

**Where it's kept:** on [Railway](https://railway.com)'s servers, where the app runs. I keep private backup copies, which I delete when they're no longer needed.

**What the app doesn't do:** it sends no email, shows no ads, has no tracking or analytics, and your data isn't sold or shared with anyone.

**Your choices:**

- You can change your profile and password at any time (Profile page).
- To have your account and data deleted, ask me: [contact]. The app can't do it by itself yet, so I'll do it by hand and confirm when it's done. Settle any open balance with your housemates first.
- Forgot your password? Ask me and I'll set a temporary one for you to change.

**Joining:** sign up at [frontend address] with the invite code [code]. Then either enter your household's join code (the owner finds it on the household page), or have the owner invite the exact email address you signed up with: the invitation appears in the app (no email is sent). You stay logged in for 7 days, then log in again.

**Feedback and questions:** [where feedback goes: a group chat, an email address, a form…]. Screenshots help.

**You must be 18 or over**, or have a parent's or guardian's agreement (below).
