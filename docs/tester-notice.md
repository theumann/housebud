# HouseBud tester notice (template)

This is the reference version, kept current with what the app does. The version actually sent to testers lives outside the repo (the owner's own document, sent by email, with a dated PDF of each copy sent). When this file changes, update that document before sending the next notice.

Send it to each tester before they sign up, with the bracketed parts filled in. Keep a copy of who received it and when, and of any parent's agreement, outside the repo: they hold names, addresses and invite codes.

---

## Welcome to the HouseBud test

Thanks for trying HouseBud. It's an app for running a shared home: chores, a shopping list, shared expenses, house rules, a calendar and a household chat. You're one of the first people using it for real, so a few things to know first.

**It's a test.** The app works, but expect rough edges, and tell me about them. Features may change, and I may need to fix or reset things. If something important goes wrong (wrong amounts in expenses, for example), let me know rather than working around it.

**What the app stores about you:**

- Your account: email address, username, name, birth date, school and year, the city and ZIP code you're looking in, and your password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it, me included).
- What you add to your profile: a display name, a short bio, a photo link.
- Your answers to the compatibility questions, if you fill them in.
- What you do in your household: chores, shopping items, expenses and payments, house rules and votes, calendar entries, and chat messages.
- Problem reports you send with the bug icon: your description, the page you were on, your browser and screen size, and, unless you untick it, a screenshot of that page. The screenshot shows the page as you saw it, so it can include housemates' names and messages. It never shows anything outside the app.

**Who sees it:**

- **Your household** sees your name and photo, and everything you add in the household (chores, expenses, chat…). People who join later don't see chat messages from before they joined.
- [While matching is closed: **Nobody else** sees your profile.] [Once matching is open: other users looking in the same area see your profile card (name, age, school, photo) and your compatibility with them.]
- **Me**, as the person running the app: I can access the database to fix problems and to take backups. I won't look at your household's content unless you ask me to, or to fix something you reported. Problem reports, screenshots included, are seen by me only.

**Where it's kept:** on [Railway](https://railway.com)'s servers, where the app runs. I keep private backup copies, which I delete when they're no longer needed, and I save the screenshots from problem reports privately on my computer to look at them.

**What the app doesn't do:** it sends no email, shows no ads, has no tracking or analytics, and your data isn't sold or shared with anyone.

**Your choices:**

- You can change your profile and password at any time (Profile page).
- To have your account and data deleted, problem reports included, ask me: privacy@housebud.app. The app can't do it by itself yet, so I'll do it by hand and confirm when it's done. Expenses and payments you shared with housemates stay in the household's records under an anonymous name, so their balances stay right; everything else is deleted. Settle any open balance with your housemates first.
- Forgot your password? Ask me and I'll set a temporary one for you to change.

**Joining:** sign up at https://housebud.app/signup?code=[code] (the link fills in the invite code). Then either enter your household's join code (the owner finds it on the household page), or have the owner invite the exact email address you signed up with: the invitation appears in the app (no email is sent). You stay logged in for 7 days, then log in again.

**Feedback and questions:** use the bug icon at the top of every page. Describe what happened; a screenshot of the page goes with it unless you untick "Include screenshot". If you can't log in or the app won't load, contact me instead: contact@housebud.app.

**You must be 18 or over**, or have a parent's or guardian's agreement (below).
